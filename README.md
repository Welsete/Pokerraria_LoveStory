Immerse yourself in the captivating crossover of Terraria and Pokémon with the Terramon Mod!

**Terramon Brasil** is a fan fork of [Terramon](https://github.com/JamzOJamz/Terramon) built on top of its turn-based battle branch. It adds a Cobblemon-style progression with trainers and gyms tied to Terraria's bosses, and a full Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR) translation.

## Features

- Over 145 Pokémon to find and catch, including their Shiny forms.
- 5 different Poké Balls with interactive mechanics.
- A brand new Town NPC, the Poké Mart Clerk.
- Custom user interface (UI) elements.
- Vanity and other decorative items.

## Terramon Brasil additions

### Journey and world
- **Professor Oak** greets new players, tells the story of Pokémon in Terraria and hands over the starter choice. Talk to him again for tips.
- **Pokémon League:** 8 Gym Leaders (Brock to Giovanni), each unlocked by a Terraria boss and the previous badge, then the Elite Four (Lorelei, Bruno, Agatha, Lance) and the Champion, Blue, after the Moon Lord. Badges, money rewards, a saved Hall of Fame, and challenge items crafted at a Work Bench to summon each leader.
- **Gym and League buildings:** new worlds generate a Pokémon Gym and a Pokémon League on the surface, plus Brock's cave gym near spawn, each with a pedestal that calls your next opponent. Pedestals can also be crafted for older worlds.
- **Walking trainers:** Brock walks around his gym and turns to face nearby players.
- **Wild Pokémon that grow with your world:** wild levels and evolution stages rise with boss progression and depth. A wild Onix attacks nearby monsters with rock spikes.
- **Pokémon passives:** your companion gives a small bonus by type, and Lucario's aura makes nearby ores glow underground. Can be turned off in the server config.
- **Fishing and items:** Old, Good and Super Rods with a Water-type fishing pool by biome, new evolution stone sources, apricorns from every tree type, and Exp. Candies from enemies and bosses.
- **22 held items** with their own sprites (Leftovers, berries, type boosters, Focus Sash, Life Orb and more), crafted at a Work Bench and used in battle.

### Battles
- **Turn-based battles** powered by Pokémon Showdown, against wild Pokémon, trainers and other players.
- **Smarter trainers:** trainers pick moves by type matchup, STAB and status, use setup and healing moves sensibly, and sometimes switch to a better Pokémon.
- **Move visuals and animations:** each move plays an effect by type and category, and the Pokémon plays a matching attack, hurt, faint or victory pose. Transform and Imposter really change Ditto's look.
- **Move details:** every move button shows type, category, power, accuracy and PP, and hovering it shows the full description.
- **Bag in battle:** use Potions, Revives and Poké Balls on your turn. A successful catch ends the battle, while a Pokémon that breaks free costs you your turn.
- **Improved catching:** catch rate rises up to 3x at low HP and with status conditions. Defeated wild Pokémon stay knocked out for a few seconds so you can still catch them.
- **Battle log in chat** and a **battle history window (L)** with the whole battle turn by turn.
- **Experience, fainting and forced switch:** defeated Pokémon give EXP, level ups are shown in chat, and the party panel opens when your active Pokémon faints.
- **Multiplayer:** challenge other players with an accept/decline popup, friendly PvP battles that heal both teams, and synced Pokémon data for everyone.

### Pokémon
- **Mystery Dungeon style sprites** from the PMD SpriteCollab for all 151 Kanto Pokémon and Lucario, with idle, walk, attack, hurt, sleep, faint, hop, lying down and more, chosen by what the Pokémon is doing. Shiny versions included.
- **Portraits** in the party sidebar and inventory slots, normal and shiny.
- **Summary screen (middle click or I):** stats, IVs and EVs as radar charts with plain explanations, ability descriptions, moves, where it was met, friendship and field abilities. Change moves here at any time to any move your Pokémon could have learned by its level.
- **Move learning and evolution:** Pokémon learn moves on level up (with a forget-a-move window) and evolve with the classic white silhouette scene (B to skip).
- **Richer Pokédex:** level-up moves, base stats, abilities with descriptions, habitat, evolutions and an animated preview.

### Other
- **Brazilian Portuguese translation** of the interface, Pokédex, moves and abilities.
- **Testing tools:** `/heal` and `/level` commands, plus a draggable admin panel (F8, Debug Mode) to heal, level up, evolve, spawn Pokémon, trainers and Professor Oak, toggle bosses and reset badges.
