Pixel-art VFX sprite sheet, original design, for a Terraria-style 2D game.

PALETTE (use only these colors, flat fills, no gradients, no glow):
#FFFFFF #A5B4FC #6366F1 #4338CA #251F72 #130D3E
Background: flat solid #FF00FF, nothing else in it.

STYLE: Terraria Dragon Armor / Betsy slash look: chunky 3-claw rake, sharp 1px darker outline.

FRAME BY FRAME:
1: Trio of sharp curved claws made of glowing indigo energy entering at upper-right (45 degrees).
2: Claws accelerate downward in a sweeping slash arc, trailing bright cyan-blue energy ribbons.
3: Impact point: claws rip through center, leaving 3 distinct white cutting scars (#FFFFFF).
4: Peak tear: cutting scars detonate into 4 flying dragon scale sparks and a horizontal energy shock.
5: Claw trails dissolve; cutting scars break into disconnected glowing energy segments.
6: Energy segments fade from indigo to deep purple (#251F72).
7: Only 4 tiny glowing draconic motes drifting upward.
8: Last frame: only 3 small fading sparks (1px each, #130D3E), dissipating into air.
