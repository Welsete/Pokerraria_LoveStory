Pokerraria: LoveStory is a Terraria and Pokémon crossover with turn-based battles, Gyms tied to Terraria's bosses and a story of its own.

Features:
- Catch, train and evolve the Kanto Pokémon, including their Shiny forms.
- Turn-based battles against wild Pokémon, trainers and Gym Leaders.
- Gym badges set how high a level your Pokémon obey, like in the main games.
- A Pokémon Center next to spawn, where Nurse Joy heals your Pokémon for free.
- Animated Pokémon, Pokédex, Summary screen, held items and much more.

The source code is available at
https://github.com/Welsete/Pokerraria_LoveStory
